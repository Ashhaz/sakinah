# Sakinah content review standard

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Do not invent a verse, hadith, Arabic phrase, narrator, source, grading or reference number.
2. Qur'an entries must identify surah and ayah, and name the English translation used. English wording is a translation of meaning, not the Arabic revelation.
3. Hadith entries must identify collection and the numbering system shown by the linked source. Add a grade only after checking a reliable hadith reference or qualified reviewer.
4. Read surrounding context before connecting a source to a modern problem.
5. Keep source text, scholarly explanation, editorial reflection and general wellbeing advice visibly distinct.
6. Never claim that dhikr or du'a guarantees the removal of depression, anxiety, insomnia or another health condition.
7. Never present mental illness as evidence of weak iman or sin.
8. Encourage appropriate clinical and emergency support when needed.
